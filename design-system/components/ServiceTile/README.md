# ServiceTile

A service tile is an entry point to a major area (Policy & Regulation, Standard, มติคณะรัฐมนตรี, Open Government Data, Citizen Portal), laid out three across as on the home page.

**Consumer provides:** a 24px single-colour icon (Font Awesome Solid or a simple SVG), a title of up to four words and a one-line Thai description.

- The icon sits on an `orange` disc and the tile on `surface-subtle`. On hover the tile lifts to `surface` with `shadow-card`.
- Keep a set of tiles parallel: all English titles or all Thai titles.
- Don't use photographs inside tiles; use NewsCard for those.
