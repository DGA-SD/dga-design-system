# SlideTitle

The title slide for decks is authored at 1920×1080 on a `navy` ground, with an orange kicker, a white `slide-hero` headline, a subtitle, brand colour blocks at top-right and the logo on a white plate.

**Consumer provides:** a kicker (organisation or event), a headline of up to eight words, a subtitle (meeting, date or period in the Buddhist Era) and, optionally, the presenter.

- Use `dga-slide dga-slide--navy`. The blocks are `orange` plus one `chart-3`, at `radius-lg` corners.
- Put no photograph behind the headline and use only one title slide per deck.
- For a light working deck, drop `--navy`. The logo then sits directly on white (use `dga-slide__logo`).
