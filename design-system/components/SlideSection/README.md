# SlideSection

The section divider is a full `orange` slide with a large navy section number and title, used between the parts of a long deck.

**Consumer provides:** a two-digit section number and a section title of up to six words.

- Text is `navy` on `orange` (4.69:1, large text). No logo, bullets or imagery.
- Use one divider per major section, or none in decks shorter than 10 slides.
