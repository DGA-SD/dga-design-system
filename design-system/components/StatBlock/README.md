# StatBlock

A stat block shows one headline number with its meaning and source, for reports, web highlights and deck summaries.

**Consumer provides:** the number (Arabic numerals, with units in the label rather than the number), a label of up to eight words and a source line.

- The number is `orange` at 56px (web) or `slide-stat` 120px (slides), which passes as large text. The label is `ink-secondary` and the source `ink-muted`.
- Show at most three or four per row, and always give a source.
- Don't invent or round numbers for effect, or use stats as decoration.
