# Footer

The site footer is a full-width `navy` band with the logo on a white plate, contact details and link columns with orange headings.

**Consumer provides:** the address and contact lines, 2–4 link columns (heading plus up to six links) and a legal line with a Buddhist Era year and privacy link.

- Text is `on-navy` and column headings are `orange` (4.69:1 on navy).
- The logo always sits on the white plate and is never placed directly on navy.
- Social links use Font Awesome brand icons in `on-navy` with `aria-label`s.
